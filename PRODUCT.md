# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users: Small and medium business (SMB) owners and startup founders in Sri Lanka (retail, hospitality, professional services, local manufacturing, commerce).
- **Situation:** Operating businesses generating revenue, but marketing is fragmented, ad boosts are wasted without predictable returns, websites look nice but fail to convert, or brand presence appears amateurish.
- **Job to be done:** Gain predictable customer enquiries, build credible brand authority, and install a reliable commercial growth system without needing expensive full-time marketing hires or dealing with disconnected freelancers.

## Product Purpose

BHive builds businesses people can understand, trust, choose, and remember. It unites business strategy, brand identity, marketing strategy, high-converting digital experiences, and content execution into a single coherent growth system. Success means delivering measurable customer enquiries, commercial clarity, and sustainable revenue growth for Sri Lankan businesses.

## Positioning

"Strategy, brand, marketing, and digital execution — built as one connected growth system."
Unlike conventional digital agencies that sell isolated tactical services (random ad boosting, disconnected graphic design, or brochure websites), BHive operates as an accountable business builder. The work is planned, commercial, and outcome-driven rather than guessed or decorative.

## Operating Context

- **Market Environment:** Sri Lankan commercial ecosystem with strong mobile usage and primary business communication via WhatsApp (`wa.me`). Multilingual audience (English, Sinhala, Tamil).
- **Engagement Funnel:** Zero-friction low-risk primary conversion: **Free Growth Consultation** triggered globally across the site via a modal offering an immediate WhatsApp chat or a lightweight lead capture form.
- **Consultation Delivery:** 15-minute diagnostic review and actionable roadmap delivered to the business owner.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **5 Core Capabilities:**
  1. Business Strategy (Customer profiles, competitor check, pricing & packaging, growth roadmap)
  2. Brand & Identity (Visual identity, typography, guidelines, social/print templates)
  3. Marketing Strategy (Customer acquisition channels, budget allocation, predictable enquiry funnels)
  4. Website & Digital Experience (Fast, mobile-first, high-converting Next.js web experiences)
  5. Content & Social (Structured content calendars, value-driven campaigns, conversion messaging)
- **Technical Capabilities:** Next.js 16.3.4 App Router, React 19, TypeScript strict, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Lenis smooth scrolling, Zod-validated lead ingestion (`/api/leads`).
- **Technical Constraints:** Fast mobile-first delivery, zero heavy WebGL, LF line endings, strict accessibility compliance.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** BHive
- **Brand Voice:** Direct, grounded, practical, commercially intelligent, and respectful of business owners' time and capital. Avoid agency jargon and fluff ("unlock potential", "next level", "360-degree", "creative solutions").
- **Visual Identity:** Modern, high-trust corporate blue and authority navy (`#010071`, `#3068f9`, `#0b4cc4`) with disciplined, sparse honey accents. Editorial layout, high-contrast typography (Inter display and text).

## Evidence on Hand

- **Strict Verified-Proof Policy:** Currently `showProof: false` in `src/config/site.ts`. All case study metrics, client names, logos, and testimonials in development are marked as illustrative concepts/demo placeholders (`isDemo: true`).
- **Policy Enforcement:** Under no circumstances should real client names, logos, metrics, revenue numbers, or testimonials be fabricated. Live proof remains hidden/gated until verified by the business owner.

## Product Principles

1. **System over Silos:** Marketing only works when strategy, brand, website, and acquisition channels are aligned to one commercial goal.
2. **Planned, Not Guessed:** Replace random ad boosts and aesthetic trends with deliberate customer research, clear value propositions, and predictable funnels.
3. **Owner-Centric Pragmatism:** Speak the language of revenue, margin, and enquiries, not designer jargon. Respect Sri Lankan business realities and commercial workflows.
4. **Radical Truth in Evidence:** Proof must be 100% verified and authentic before being published. No fake claims, manufactured stats, or artificial urgency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Mobile-first responsiveness tested for high-volume mobile traffic.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` across all UI animations.
- High-contrast text compliance (WCAG AA/AAA).
- Multi-language communication awareness for the Sri Lankan market (English, Sinhala, Tamil support indicated).
